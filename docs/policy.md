# Payout policy (pasted from the ops wiki)

- Every completed trip pays ₹25 base + ₹6 for each km after the first 2 km. If the trip had a surge multiplier, it applies to the whole trip fare (base + distance). Round each trip to the nearest rupee, 0.5 rounds up.
- Daily incentive of ₹150 for 12 or more trips in a day.
- ₹10 penalty for every trip the rider cancels. Customer cancellations earn nothing.
- "Day" means the calendar day in IST.
- We only look at disputes for the last 7 days.
